# Backpack: demo script + notes

**One-liner:** Backpack is the study buddy that remembers your homework. Snap a worksheet, and it remembers what's due and what it's about. When a test is coming, it makes a study plan and a practice quiz built from YOUR homework, not generic questions.

**Who it's for:** my friend, who can't keep track of homework, studying, and time.

## 2-minute demo (do it in this order)

1. **The problem (15 sec).** "My friend has homework in 6 classes, tests popping up, and keeps forgetting what's due and what to study. Every study app makes you type everything in, so nobody keeps using it."
2. **Snap it (30 sec).** Go to **Add**, upload a photo of a real worksheet (or paste a teacher's message), tap **Remember it**. Show it pull out the title, class, due date, and topics. Fix a date, then **Save**.
3. **Today (20 sec).** Show the countdown to the next test, "due this week," and **Tonight's plan**, which mixes homework due soon with study sessions for upcoming tests.
4. **Tests (20 sec).** Open **Tests**. Show "Using X saved homeworks" and the day-by-day study plan leading up to test day.
5. **The wow moment (30 sec).** Tap **Practice quiz from my homework**. Answer a couple of questions. Point at "📎 From: [worksheet name]" under the answer: the quiz came from the homework you just snapped. Finish and show the score + "Study these next."
6. **Close (5 sec).** "Backpack remembers everything so my friend just opens it and knows what to do tonight."

## Before you demo

- Open the link **on claude.ai while signed in.** The AI (reading homework, making quizzes) and saving only work there.
- The first time you tap **Remember it** or **Quiz**, it asks permission to use AI. Say yes **before** the demo so it doesn't pop up live.
- **Load sample week** (on the empty Today screen) fills in Bio, Precalc, and English examples. Good backup if live AI is slow. Then add one real item live.
- Bring 1–2 real worksheet photos (no names or personal info on them).
- If AI ever fails, **Add one by hand instead** still works, so the demo never dies.

## What it does (feature list for the submission)

- Add homework by photo or pasted text; AI extracts every assignment, test, quiz, or project with class, due date, topics, and study notes
- Edit anything before saving; add by hand without AI
- Today: next test countdown, due this week, late items, tonight's plan
- Tests: day-by-day study plan up to test day, linked to related saved homework
- Practice quizzes generated from the student's own saved homework, with explanations, "from" source, score, and weak topics
- Check off finished work; everything saves to your account

## Known limits

- AI and saving need claude.ai sign-in (judges watching your screen is fine; a logged-out visitor sees "AI off").
- Max 4 photos at once.
