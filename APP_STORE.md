## Description (max 4000)
Meet Rilo, a round little panda in a green headband who helps you scroll less and do more of what you actually care about.

Most screen-time apps just lock you out. Rilo asks what you're opening the app for, gives you a fair number of opens, and when you hit your limit, he helps you figure out what to do instead.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick the apps that eat your day. Rilo shows you what they really cost: days a year, not minutes.
• Tell Rilo what you'd rather spend that time on: the gym, reading, music, friends, school.
• Rilo builds your plan: limits that make sense, plus what to do instead.

OPENS, NOT LOCKS
• Each limited app gets a few opens a day, with a set number of minutes each.
• Opening an app asks one quick question: what is it for? Pick a quick open (half) or a full one.
• Leave early and it only costs half an open.
• One emergency pass a day, for when you really need it.

A BUDDY, NOT A BOSS
• Rilo's mood follows your day. Stay under your goal and he's proud. Go over and he lets you know.
• Earn XP for every day under your goal, level up, and unlock 23 Rilos and 5 themes.
• Weekly step-down: Rilo tightens one small thing a week, so it never feels like a cliff.
• Nightly check-in: tell Rilo how the day went (talk or type) and he plans tomorrow around it.
• Buddy mode: friends see Rilo's mood and your streak, never your numbers.

FOR WHEN YOU MEAN IT
• Strict mode: no half opens, no borrowing, and a 24-hour wait before you can loosen anything. Getting stricter is always instant.
• Creator Mode: posting isn't scrolling. Say what you're there to post and for how long, and Rilo tracks it separately.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything Rilo knows stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The mic is only on while you hold the talk button, and your words are turned into text on your phone.

# Privacy policy (draft)

*Last updated: [date]*

Rilo is a screen-time buddy app. This policy explains what Rilo stores and what it doesn't. The short version: **everything stays on your phone.**

**What Rilo stores, and where**
Rilo saves what you tell it so it can coach you: your first name, the apps you picked, your limits and plan, your daily goal, your XP, level, streak, chosen Rilo and theme, your settings (bedtime, school hours, check-in time, strict mode, Creator Mode), your nightly check-in answers and the tasks Rilo pulls from them, and today's opens and minutes. All of it is saved **only on your phone**, inside the app. Rilo has no accounts and no servers, and it doesn't send this information anywhere. Deleting the app, or tapping "start over" in the You tab, deletes it.

**Rilo's thinking and memory**
On phones with Apple Intelligence, Rilo uses Apple's on-device intelligence to reply to you, plan your day and read your nightly check-in, and he remembers the useful things you tell him (like your hobbies, goals and what's coming up). This all happens and stays on your phone; nothing is sent to Apple, to Rilo or to anyone else. You can see and delete what he remembers any time in You ▸ what rilo knows.

**Screen Time**
When Rilo blocks or limits apps, it uses Apple's Screen Time tools. Apple designed these so that Rilo doesn't see which apps you use or your browsing; it only gets anonymous tokens to put a shield on the apps you picked. That data stays on your phone.

**Microphone and speech**
Talking to Rilo is optional. The microphone is only on **while you hold the talk button**. What you say is turned into text **on your phone** using Apple's speech recognition. Rilo doesn't record or keep audio, and doesn't send it anywhere. You can always type instead, and you can turn the mic off any time in Settings.

**Notifications**
Rilo's reminders (like the nightly check-in) are scheduled on your phone. No notification server is involved.

**Purchases**
Rilo Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; Rilo never sees your payment details. Rilo only learns whether you have Rilo Pro.

**Buddies**
If you use buddy mode, a friend you invite with a code sees only Rilo's mood and your streak, never your screen time numbers.

**Children**
Rilo is meant for people aged 13 and up. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because nothing leaves the phone.

**No tracking, no ads**
Rilo doesn't track you across apps or websites, doesn't show ads, and doesn't use analytics services.
